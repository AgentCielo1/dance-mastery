# Oceania Partnerships — the packs we will not build alone

This document extends the respect architecture of
[AFRICAN-PARTNERSHIPS.md](AFRICAN-PARTNERSHIPS.md) to the Pacific. The
principle is unchanged and worth restating whole:

> **An empty node with a named plan is worth more than a full node that
> steals.**

The Oceania wave shipped **one** pack — 'Ori Tahiti, as a foundation, because
its own global school scene is led by Tahitian teachers and several teach
online from Tahiti itself. The other three forms in the catalog's Oceanian
family are **community-held**, and this app builds no packs for them. This
file is the plan for what happens instead.

## The community-held forms

### Hula (Kahiko & 'Auana) — Hawai'i
- **Why no pack:** hula is taught in the hālau under a kumu hula — a lineage
  system with protocols ('ūniki), chant, and genealogy at its center. Kahiko
  is sacred practice. Even 'auana, the modern form, belongs to the hālau
  system. An app teaching hula steps outside that system would be exactly the
  costume-first extraction this project exists to refuse.
- **The partnership path:** a hula pack exists only if a kumu hula or hālau
  chooses to author it — their curriculum, their sequencing, their protocols
  stated their way, their name on every screen, revenue shared, and the pack
  withdrawn at their word. The catalog entry stands as the invitation.
- **What the app does today:** the catalog names hula with respect, flags it
  community-held, and points learners toward hālau — including the many that
  welcome beginners across Hawai'i and the diaspora.

### Haka — Aotearoa (Māori)
- **Why no pack:** haka are ancestral taonga of iwi — challenge, welcome,
  mourning, identity. Specific haka belong to specific iwi (Ka Mate to Ngāti
  Toa, recognized in New Zealand law). Kapa haka is taught in rōpū within
  Māori communities and schools. None of that is an app's to teach.
- **The partnership path:** authored by a kapa haka rōpū or Māori cultural
  organization or not at all — same terms as above.
- **What the app does today:** community-held flag, honest catalog entry,
  and the pointer that kapa haka festivals (Te Matatini above all) are the
  tradition's own stage — watch, and understand whose it is.

### Poi — Aotearoa (Māori)
- **Why no pack:** poi is a discipline within kapa haka, held in the same
  rōpū system. (The globally-marketed "poi spinning" prop-flow scene is a
  separate, derived practice; this app does not conflate the two, and a
  future flow-arts entry would credit the Māori origin explicitly.)
- **The partnership path and today's stance:** as with haka.

## Terms (identical to the African brief)

1. **Authorship on-screen** — the tradition-bearer's name and organization on
   every lesson, not in a credits footer.
2. **Revenue share** — if this project ever earns, the authoring communities
   earn first.
3. **Capture on their terms** — their teachers, their protocols, their veto
   at every stage, including after publication.
4. **Context in their words** — history and meaning written by the
   community, not summarized about them.
5. **The right to say no** — a declined invitation closes the matter. The
   catalog entry remains, respectfully, as it is.

## Sequencing

No outreach happens until this project has something real to offer:
a working platform (done), a demonstrated respect architecture (done —
16 honesty gates, 44 packs of credited curriculum), and a founder ready to
make the ask in person. When that outreach begins, it starts with the
communities' own cultural organizations, never with individual artists
approached cold.

Until then: the empty nodes stand, named, as promises.
